## How the calculator reads the rules (assumptions still to confirm)

- Colour rules: planet sitting in a trigger zodiac = weight 1.0; planet aspecting a trigger zodiac from elsewhere = `ASPECT_W` 0.5 (the owner gave no figure for the colour rules).
- D1 Final = 0.5 D1 + 0.25 KP + 0.25 Bhav Chalit per (zodiac, rule). If no birth data is given, KP is skipped, the weights are renormalised, and the output says so.
- Bhav Chalit = equal house, each house spans cusp +/- 15 deg, cusp 1 = lagna longitude. KP = sidereal Placidus, ayanamsa calibrated from the site's own lagna.
- Several negatives in one zodiac: colours from the use lists minus every avoid list; blends proposed (blue+yellow=green, red+yellow=orange, red+blue=purple, black+white=grey, ...); a blend that lands on an avoided colour is rejected.
- D9 check: the rules are applied to D9 signs; colours anti in D9 for the same zodiac are removed from the D1 Final advice.
- Aspect %: P1 = owner ladder, "+/- 5/10/15 %" read as percent OF the aspect angle (5% of 180 = 9 deg); P2 = western (orbs 8/6/8/8/8, linear); P3 = Vedic (full on the owner's list and conjunction, Parashara partials 25/50/75 for the seven classical planets); P4 = Lal Kitab house table from Goswami pp.118-124 (see OPEN_QUESTIONS).
- House-by-house table (`house_final_table`): owner confirmed on 23 Nov 2026 that the D9 check is made HOUSE BY HOUSE (D9 house h counted from the D9 lagna, compared with D1 house h). D1 Final is the base; D9 can only veto colours, never add them to a house where D1 has a rule; where D1 has no rule but D9 has, the advice is labelled D9-only. `colour_advice` still compares by zodiac (older view); the house table is the owner-confirmed one.
- House report: constant houses, moving zodiacs. Net = classical dignity of planets in the house (+) minus the owner negative load of the zodiac in that house (D1 Final weights of the 15 rules). Verdict supportive > +0.25, strained < -0.25.
- Remedy: colour covering the most rule weight, ties go to the owner's listed order; a blend only when two or more rules meet in one zodiac and the mix is not avoided. Owner Principle 1 body parts per house; Lal Kitab planet body parts for the planets involved.
- Exhaustive check (`enumerate_all`): 12 zodiacs x every subset of 12 planets = 49,152 cases, 33,760 with at least one rule: every case has a colour that is on no avoid list, 29,792 have one colour good for every planet involved, 0 violations. `data/owner_house_zodiac_planet.csv` = all 1,728 house x zodiac x planet rows.
