## Useful side facts from Gautam on the zone codes themselves (his claims, not about signs)
- G04-08-032: W6 is in the west / north-west area (thyroid claim). G02-14-055: "n1" is the north-west zone (Roga). So W6 and N1 are neighbours, next to Taurus's W7, W8, N1. This hints the unassigned W6 may belong with Taurus, but that is a guess, not a source.
- G04-10-036: E8 and S1 are the south-east pair (Antariksha, Anil). This matches Pisces having E7, E8, S1 near "ESE/SE".
- G05-14-028: E2 is the Parjanya zone; G01-18-019 places E3 near the NNW (the note says "probably", so unreliable). E3 near NNW would conflict with Leo's ENE; to confirm.
